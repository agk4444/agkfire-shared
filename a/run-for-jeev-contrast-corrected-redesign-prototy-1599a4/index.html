Our mission

No struggle is too great to overcome.

Our mission is to support and promote mental health awareness and suicide prevention in teens and young adults, creating a foundation for the youth of today to live in a better tomorrow.

Through the annual Run for Jeev, community-centered mental health events, and community partnerships, we strive to create a supportive space where people feel seen, heard, and understood — reminding them that they are never alone in their journey.

By encouraging open conversations and promoting hope, we believe that no struggle is too great to overcome and no thought should go unshared. Together, we can support one another and help prevent suicide among young adults.

What changed in this prototype

Every inline link moved from the failing #7FB432 to #4A6B1F — 6.10:1, comfortably past AA.

The donate button moved from #EB9309 to #8A5200 — white text on it now measures 6.39:1.

Four font families cut to two. Barlow for body, Oswald for headings. Roboto and Montserrat dropped — they were near-substitutes for Barlow and added two network round-trips on the critical path.

For whoever edits Elementor

The four edits

1 · Global colors — the whole fix

Elementor → Site Settings → Global Colors. Change the primary and accent swatches; most of the page follows.

Primary   #EB9309  →  #8A5200     (2.41:1 → 6.39:1 with white)
Accent    #7FB432  →  #4A6B1F     (2.45:1 → 6.10:1 on background)

2 · Fonts — cut four to two

Keep Barlow for body and Oswald for headings. Delete Roboto and Montserrat from every widget that references them.

Body     Barlow      (keep)
Heading  Oswald      (keep)
Body     Roboto      (delete — near-substitute for Barlow)
Body     Montserrat  (delete — near-substitute for Barlow)

3 · Alt text — the tedious one

Manual, per image, no shortcut. The hero and carousel currently render alt="", and DSC_6165 is a camera filename, not a description. Copy the alt text written in the feed section above as a starting pattern.

4 · OG image — highest social ROI

Confirmed absent from the live site. For an org whose strategy is Instagram reposts, every shared link currently renders as a bare URL. Any 1200×630 image fixes all of them at once.

og:image       https://runforjeev.com/wp-content/uploads/og/runforjeev-1200x630.jpg
og:image:width 1200
og:image:height 630
og:title       Run for Jeev — mental health awareness & suicide prevention
og:description 5th Annual Run for Jeev · November 29, 2026 · The Woodlands, TX

Free wins, no design decision needed

Hide or delete the comment-spam honeypot currently rendering as visible footer text · change ©2022 to ©2026 · title-case the donate button. Under an hour total.
