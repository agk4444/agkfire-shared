How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.
Green — correct letter, right spot.
Yellow — right letter, wrong spot.
Gray — letter is not in the word.
Duplicates score in two passes: greens lock first, so a second copy of a letter only turns yellow if a spare copy is left.
◆ Hard mode — you must keep every revealed green in place and reuse every yellow.
