How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries.
Green — correct letter, right spot.
Yellow — right letter, wrong spot.
Gray — letter is not in the word.
Duplicates are scored in two passes: greens are locked first, so a second copy of a letter is only yellow if a spare copy remains.
